What KELP does

Your sources, zero lock-in

Connect Stremio or JSON add-ons, a home NAS via WebDAV, or local folders. Streams from every source merge into a single ranked picker with automatic resolution, HDR, and audio language detection.

Offline downloads for full seasons

Save a movie, an episode, or an entire season before traveling — complete with subtitles and artwork. Downloaded files automatically take priority even without a connection.

Encrypted sync without an account

No sign-up or passwords. Pair devices with a QR code so watch progress, profiles, collections, and add-ons stay in sync — and send playback from your phone straight to your TV in one tap.

Live TV with EPG & catch-up

Add your own M3U or Xtream playlist along with an XMLTV guide. Browse what is currently airing and jump back into channel catch-up archives from one screen.

Built-in player that stays out of your way

Auto frame rate matching (AFR), Skip Intro, Up Next auto-play, customizable subtitle styling, ambient pause showcase, and Picture-in-Picture with touch gestures on mobile.

Tailored to every screen

Remote-first navigation on Android TV, touch controls on phones and tablets, and mouse, touch-laptop, and HiDPI support on Windows and Linux — with separate adult and kids profiles.
