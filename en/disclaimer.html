Part II — Privacy Policy (GDPR)

This section explains how data is handled when using the KELP Application and kelp.page in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Czech Act No. 110/2019 Coll. on Personal Data Processing.

11. Data Controller

The data controller within the meaning of Article 4(7) GDPR is the developer of KELP operating under the pseudonym LUKYMAS. Privacy contact e-mail: mail@lukymas.eu.org.

12. Data Minimization & Local-First Storage

KELP is built on a privacy-by-design architecture. It requires no registration, maintains no user accounts, and operates no central user database. Operational data is stored locally in an SQLite database on your device:

  • Profiles & settings: user-chosen profile names, profile type, language, appearance, player and subtitle preferences.
  • Library & watch history: watched titles, playback positions, custom collections, and search history.
  • Source & add-on configuration: add-on definitions, Live TV playlist URLs, and optional add-on or Trakt.tv credentials stored locally on your device.
  • Offline content & cache: downloaded media files, subtitles, artwork, and temporary metadata cache.

13. Optional End-to-End Encrypted Sync (Zero-Knowledge)

Synchronizing your library and settings across your own devices is completely optional and only activated when you explicitly pair devices:

  • Local LAN P2P sync: transfers data directly between devices on your local network without involving external servers (including local “Send to TV” remote open).
  • Remote zero-knowledge relay (e.g., relay.kelp.page or your self-hosted instance): before leaving your device, sync payloads are compressed (GZIP) and encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM with a key that never leaves your devices.
  • The relay server stores no names, e-mail addresses, or readable content — only a random anonymous vault identifier (vaultId) and opaque encrypted blobs (max. 512 KiB per device). The controller has no technical ability to decrypt payloads or link them to an individual.
  • Encrypted relay blobs expire automatically after 180 days of inactivity; one-time 6-character pairing slots expire after at most 5 minutes or immediately upon retrieval.

14. Direct Communication with Third-Party Services (Independent Controllers)

Your device may communicate directly with the following external services, each acting as an independent data controller under its own privacy policy (including standard processing of your IP address and HTTP headers):

  • TMDb (The Movie Database): search queries and retrieval of catalog metadata and artwork.
  • Trakt.tv (optional): watch history synchronization only if you explicitly connect your account.
  • Discord Rich Presence (optional, Desktop only): local IPC communication with your installed Discord client to display the currently playing title.
  • User-configured add-on, WebDAV, and IPTV servers: requests go exclusively to endpoints you configure yourself. The Developer is not responsible for how those servers process data.
  • Cloudflare / GitHub Pages: hosting infrastructure for kelp.page and the optional sync relay (standard infrastructure network logs).

15. Legal Bases for Processing (Article 6 GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract / delivering a requested feature (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): local storage of preferences and history, and relaying encrypted blobs when sync is enabled.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): opting into remote relay sync or linking optional external accounts (withdrawable at any time in Settings).
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): maintaining technical security and abuse prevention on the optional sync relay (rate-limiting and service integrity).
