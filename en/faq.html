Frequently Asked Questions

Plain answers on how KELP works, what sources it supports, and how it handles your data.

What is KELP and does it include any movies or shows?

No. KELP is an open media center that pairs catalog metadata from TMDb with your own media sources and plays them in a built-in player.

Out of the box it contains no media, no pre-installed add-ons, and no built-in third-party hosting services.

What media sources can I connect?

KELP works exclusively through general, open protocols:

Movies & series: Stremio v3 (manifest.json), declarative JSON add-ons, WebDAV servers, and local disk folders.

Live TV: M3U playlists, Xtream Codes, and XMLTV EPG guides including archive catch-up.

See the add-on documentation for the full JSON add-on specification.

How does sync work between phone, TV, and computer?

KELP requires no account, e-mail, or password. Pair devices by scanning a QR code or entering a 6-character code.

Watch progress, profiles, collections, add-ons, and Live TV sources are encrypted locally with AES-256-GCM. Sync works directly over your home LAN (including one-tap Send to TV) and optionally over a zero-knowledge relay while away from home.

Does KELP support offline viewing?

Yes. You can download movies, individual episodes, or whole seasons (including an unwatched-only option) together with subtitles and artwork. Local copies automatically take priority on playback.

Storage limits, download concurrency, and Wi-Fi-only downloads on mobile are configurable in Settings.

Which platforms and languages are supported?

KELP provides dedicated native apps for Android TV, Android phones and tablets (both publicly available on Google Play), Windows 10/11, and Linux.

The interface and catalog support English, Czech, and Slovak.

Do I need to install extra codecs or player libraries on desktop?

Windows: No — the player library (libmpv-2.dll) is bundled directly inside both the MSI installer and the portable ZIP.

Linux: If mpv is not already installed on your system, install mpv-libs (Fedora) or libmpv2 (Debian / Ubuntu).

Does KELP collect personal data or show ads?

No. KELP contains no ads, analytics, cookies, or tracking. All app data stays locally on your device. Read the full details in our Legal Notice & Privacy Policy.
