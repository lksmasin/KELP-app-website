KELP 7.0.0-beta.1

November 2026
  • All-new native foundation: faster startup, smoother browsing, and a unified codebase across Android TV, Android phones and tablets, Windows, and Linux.
  • Expanded Home, Movies & Series discovery: new curated rows (Now in Theaters, Upcoming, On the Air, Offline Ready), live add-on catalogs, and interactive genre filter chips.
  • In-app update checker & diagnostics: Beta and Stable update channels with direct downloads, secret-redacted diagnostic log export, and startup Crash Reporter.
  • External player rescue: long-press / right-click in the stream picker or use the player error screen to hand off playback to VLC, mpv, MX Player, or Nova.
  • Desktop ergonomics: multi-monitor window state persistence, native OS media keys (Linux MPRIS2 & Windows global hotkeys), mouse wheel volume, and display sleep inhibition.
  • Account-free encrypted sync, offline downloads & Live TV: QR / 6-character pairing, Send to TV, offline movie/season downloads, and Live TV with XMLTV EPG & catch-up.
