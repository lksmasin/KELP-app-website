KELP 7.0.0-beta.1

November 2026
  • All-new native foundation: faster startup, smoother browsing, and a unified codebase across Android TV, Android phones and tablets, Windows, and Linux.
  • Dedicated Android phone & tablet app: touch-first interface with player gestures (brightness, volume, seek), Picture-in-Picture (PiP), and a built-in QR scanner for pairing.
  • Account-free encrypted sync: link devices with a QR or 6-character code to sync profiles, watch history, collections, and add-ons — including one-tap Send to TV from mobile.
  • Offline downloads: save movies, individual episodes, or full seasons with subtitles and artwork, with local files taking automatic priority offline.
  • Redesigned stream picker & open sources: automatic detection of resolution, HDR/Dolby Vision, audio tracks, and languages; support for Stremio v3, declarative JSON v2 add-ons, WebDAV, and local folders.
  • Live TV with EPG & catch-up: support for M3U and Xtream playlists, XMLTV program guides, and channel archive catch-up playback.
  • Ambient screensaver, profiles & languages: ambient screensaver on idle and pause for TV and Desktop, touch-drag gestures for desktop tablets, adult and kids profile management, and full Czech, Slovak, and English localization.
